I am a final-year undergraduate student in Information Technology at the Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

Previously, I interned as a software engineer at Metry AI and The Tinkering Society, as an AI engineer at QuarterMill AI, and as an undergraduate researcher at my institute. My work has covered a Next.js migration across five locales, an automated polling and settlement workflow with ethers.js, and verification of LLM output against source documents with Pydantic, Instructor, and Langfuse.

My work focuses on making AI agents dependable:

- Tracing agent failures back to recorded evidence ([Agent-Causality]({{ '/projects/agent-causality/' | relative_url }}))
- Grouping recurring failures from session replay and telemetry ([Vigil]({{ '/projects/vigil/' | relative_url }}))
- Restricting what agents and MCP servers can reach ([Agentic Firewall]({{ '/projects/agentic_firewall/' | relative_url }}))
- Checking claims against retrieved evidence ([VeriFact]({{ '/projects/verifact/' | relative_url }}))
